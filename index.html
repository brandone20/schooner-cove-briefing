Briefing

There's a strong wind warning in effect for the Strait of Georgia north of Nanaimo, issued at 4 pm. Right now wind is SE to ESE and gusty across the board - Sisters is reading 22 gusting 26, Entrance 18 gusting 22, and Ballenas lighter at 9 but still gusting to 23. Every zone is stuck in the same rough-but-doable band through the rest of the afternoon, with the best of it being right now before dusk. Rain is expected tonight as the wind eases to SE 10 to 20 near midnight.

ECMWF, GFS and HRDPS are calling 15+ kt at Ballenas from 4 pm, up to 24 kt. Treat that as reliable: 15+ calls from ECMWF and GFS have come true 87% and 89% of the time. At that strength they have been running light: GFS by about 2 kt and HRDPS by about 2 kt. ECMWF has been about right. (Last 14 days, 0 to 6 hours out, at Ballenas.)

ECMWF, GFS and HRDPS are calling 15+ kt at Sisters from 4 pm, up to 24 kt. Treat that as reliable: 15+ calls from ECMWF and GFS have come true 95% and 97% of the time. At that strength they have been running light: ECMWF by about 5 kt, GFS by about 5 kt and HRDPS by about 5 kt. (Last 14 days, 0 to 6 hours out, at Sisters.)

Sunday: Sunday brings rain changing to showers in the morning, with wind dropping from the SE overnight and settling into NW 5 to 15 by afternoon. Models don't agree on how much wind is left early in the day - some show it nearly calm, others still breezy - so it's worth a recheck closer to the time, though the afternoon should be easy going if the light NW wind holds.

Monday: Monday is shaping up sunny with a high of 12 and NW wind around 10 to 20 knots per the marine outlook, which would make for a comfortable day. It's still a few days out with only the coarser models covering it, so take this as an early look.
