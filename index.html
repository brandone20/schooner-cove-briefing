Briefing

Wind overnight swung around the west and northwest, gusting as high as 20 knots at Entrance around 2am, but it has eased off since then — by 4am Ballenas read 10 knots gusting 13, Entrance 7 gusting 15, and Sisters down to 5 gusting 6. The forecast has wind staying light through the day, with the morning drizzle ending and fog patches clearing by late morning. It should be a comfortable day on the water, with light wind expected to hold into the afternoon.

Sunday: Sunday stays light too, generally under 10 knots from the west or northwest through the day. Rain moves in later Sunday and overnight, and a stronger northeast outflow near 20 knots is forecast near the mainland inlets late in the day.

Monday: Early look at Monday: the marine forecast calls for easterly wind of 20 to 30 knots with a windy, rainy day, though the available models are only showing roughly 5 to 15 knots at Ballenas. Either way, expect rain and a noticeably rougher, breezier day than Saturday or Sunday.
